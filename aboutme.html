ABOUT

Engineering foundations.
Planning-led thinking.

I approach construction planning as the process of turning scope, sequence, progress, and constraints into information that teams can use.

MY APPROACH

Make the plan useful to the people delivering the work.

My civil engineering background shapes how I understand construction: activities are connected, interfaces matter, and a programme is useful when it reflects the work being delivered. My planning focus includes schedule development, progress updates, schedule reporting, coordination, and reviewing deviations.

My professional experience includes project planning for data center construction, contract and schedule administration, civil engineering, and construction delivery in international environments. I also explore digital tools and data analysis that can support consistent reporting and make project information easier to understand.

I aim to keep developing the combination of construction knowledge, project controls, and digital capability that supports complex construction environments.
